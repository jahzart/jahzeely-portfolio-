Lumgoals · Dreamscapes

A library of dreamy AI-generated scenes and loops created for the Lumgoals manifestation app and its social media.

Each scene works as a visual affirmation: a woman sitting on a glittering moon, a waterfall of violet light, a heart-shaped traffic light, a ferris wheel at night or a cozy desk at sunset, all in the soft violet and pink palette of the brand.

Visualize it, then live it

Every image was generated in vertical and horizontal formats and animated into a short loop, ready to become reels with affirmations and voice-over.
