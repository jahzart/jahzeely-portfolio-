Volantina Voladora

My children's book: I wrote it, illustrated it and designed it, from the cover to the inside pages. Available on Amazon.

When a kite escapes into the wind, Volantina flies too. Her journey takes her to a place where memories come to life and the sky seems to hold answers. Among books that breathe and creatures that defy forgetting, she meets Leo, a boy who is also looking for his way. Together they discover that sometimes the real flight begins when you stop running and dare to look inside yourself.
