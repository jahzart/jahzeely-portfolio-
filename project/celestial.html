Celestial Cards

A collection of ten illustrated cards inspired by the Sun, the Moon and the planets, in an art nouveau style, created as NFTs.

Each card gives a celestial body its own personality: a portrait on the front and an ornamental back built from the symbol of that planet, with colors and patterns that tell its story.
