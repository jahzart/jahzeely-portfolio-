Asimov Branding

Complete visual identity for a forward-thinking technology brand inspired by science fiction and rational thinking.

Asimov is a technology brand named after the legendary science fiction author Isaac Asimov. The challenge was to create a visual identity that felt both intellectual and modern, a brand that could live in the world of cutting-edge tech while honoring the heritage of rational, forward-thinking ideas.

Client
Asimov
Service
Branding
Year
2024
View on Behance

A brand built for the future

The final identity balances geometric precision with a sense of motion and discovery. A restrained color palette, custom typography treatment and a mark that references both the letter 'A' and a stylized orbit make this brand immediately recognizable.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Photoshop
  • + InDesign

Creative process

  1. 1. Research & Strategy. Deep dive into the brand's values, target audience and competitive landscape to establish a clear creative direction.
  2. 2. Concept Development. Exploration of multiple visual concepts through sketches, moodboards and initial digital explorations.
  3. 3. Refinement & Delivery. Final polish of chosen concept, building the full brand system with typography, color, iconography and usage guidelines.
