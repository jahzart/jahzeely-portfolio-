High-End Boat Detailing

Social media content for High-End Boat Detailing, a premium boat cleaning and detailing service in Puerto Rico.

Posts and stories that sell a premium service through the sea itself: aerial yacht shots, turquoise water and a bold voice that invites owners to book their next wash.

Client
High-End Boat Detailing LLC
Service
Social Media
Year
2023

Every detail, every moment

A navy, turquoise and gold palette with a strong geometric typeface and outlined repeated headlines gives the brand a luxurious, nautical look.

Tools used
  • + Photoshop
  • + Illustrator
  • + Canva

Creative process

  1. 1. Brand voice. Short, confident headlines in English and Spanish focused on shine, care and trust.
  2. 2. Art direction. Selection of aerial and lifestyle photography that puts the boats and the sea at the center.
  3. 3. Templates. Feed and story layouts with the logo, the gold accent and a clear call to action.
