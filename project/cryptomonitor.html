Cryptomonitor

A complete graphic identity and web design for a cryptocurrency monitoring platform, merging trust with cutting-edge digital aesthetics.

Cryptomonitor is a real-time cryptocurrency monitoring platform that needed a brand presence as dynamic as the market it tracks. The identity had to convey both technological sophistication and the reliability users need when managing financial data.

Client
Cryptomonitor
Service
Branding & Web
Year
2021
View on Behance

Trust in the world of crypto

A deep navy and electric blue palette, geometric precision in the logomark and a clean dashboard-inspired web design give Cryptomonitor an authoritative yet approachable presence in the fintech space.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Photoshop
  • + Figma
  • + After Effects

Creative process

  1. 1. Brand Strategy. Positioning research in the fintech/crypto space, defining brand pillars of trust, speed and transparency.
  2. 2. Identity Design. Logo system, color palette, typography and iconography for digital interfaces and marketing materials.
  3. 3. Web Design. Full website design including landing page, dashboard UI concepts and responsive mobile layouts.
