Intylact · Florece en calma

A conceptual campaign for Intylact intimate hydrating gel, created with AI: photorealistic photography and animated loops that keep the real product intact.

The concept speaks about intimate care with freshness and serenity instead of taboo: clear water, open skies and soft pink and yellow flowers surround the product, echoing its salmon tube and pink to lilac box.

Client
Intylact (concept)
Service
AI Photography & Video
Year
2026

Bloom in calm

Starting from my first concept images, I refined the art direction to make every shot photorealistic and to keep the packaging exactly as it is, with legible labels, from the product on the water to a self-care moment and the gel texture.

Tools used
  • + Magnific
  • + Nano Banana Pro
  • + Kling
  • + Photoshop
Photography

Creative process

  1. 1. Concept. Moodboard and first ideas: water, sky and flowers as a metaphor for freshness and calm.
  2. 2. AI photography. Photorealistic shots generated with the real packaging as reference, keeping every printed word.
  3. 3. Animation. Seamless vertical loops for reels and stories.
