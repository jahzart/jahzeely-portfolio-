Frens

Landing page for Frens, an app to make real plans with your friends, with a playful, bold and interactive design.

The site tells the story of the app in a few scenes: a hero full of chat bubbles and photos that react to the mouse, the philosophy behind it, plans in action and three simple steps to get started.

Creative process

  1. 1. Concept. Narrative around getting out of the screen and making plans with real friends.

  3. 3. Interaction. Mouse-reactive hero, scroll animations, light and dark mode and bilingual content.
