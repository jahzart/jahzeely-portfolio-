Period App

A compassionate and intuitive UI design for a women's health tracking app focused on clarity, empathy and ease of use.

Period App is a menstrual health tracking application designed with empathy at its core. The brief was to create an interface that felt safe, non-clinical and genuinely supportive, a digital companion that women would want to open every day.

Client
Period
Service
UI / App Design
Year
2022
View on Behance

Health tech with a human touch

The design system prioritizes soft rounded forms, a warm but clean color palette and micro-interactions that feel encouraging rather than alarming. Every screen was user-tested and refined to ensure the experience felt natural and trustworthy.

Tools used
  • + Figma
  • + Photoshop
  • + Illustrator

Creative process

  1. 1. User Research. Surveys, interviews and competitive analysis to understand what women actually need from a health tracking app.
  2. 2. Wireframes & Prototyping. Low and high-fidelity wireframes, interactive prototypes and user flow mapping for key app screens.
  3. 3. UI Design & Testing. Full visual design system, component library, and iterative user testing to validate design decisions.
