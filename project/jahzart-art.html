Jahzart Lettering Art

A series of illustrations with groovy lettering and positive messages, created for prints and merch under my brand Jahzart.

Each piece pairs a hand-drawn illustration with warped, retro lettering: a lucky cat, a crystal ball, a butterfly, a girl with daisies and boots full of flowers.

Client
Jahzart
Service
Illustration
Year
2023

Good vibes, hand drawn

Saturated purples, pinks and greens with paper textures give the series a nostalgic, 70s-inspired look that works great on prints, stickers and apparel.

Tools used
  • + Procreate
  • + Photoshop

Creative process

  1. 1. Messages. Choosing short, positive phrases for each piece.
  2. 2. Illustration. Hand drawing and coloring with paper textures.
  3. 3. Lettering. Warped retro lettering composed around each illustration.
