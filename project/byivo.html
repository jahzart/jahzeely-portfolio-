By Ivo

Hand-lettered logo for By Ivo, a lettering and creative products brand.

The logo is drawn by hand: a bold, flowing "ivo" with paint splashes that bring the energy of the brush into the mark, and a small golden "by" on top.

Client
Ivony Nuez
Service
Logo
Year
2022

Lettering with a splash of joy

A warm palette of pink, orange, cream and gold connects the logo with the colorful lettering of the products, from business cards to phone cases.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Procreate
  • + Photoshop

Creative process

  1. 1. Proposals. Several lettering directions presented to the client.
  2. 2. Final logo. Refinement of the chosen lettering and color versions for every background.
  3. 3. Applications. Brandboard and applications on signage, packaging, stationery and merch.
