Pack Iconos Gatitos

A pack of 10 kawaii kitten icons for Instagram highlights, e-commerce, apps and websites.

Each icon shows a kitten in a different everyday scene: a stroller, a gift box, a carrier, a brush, toys and more, all drawn with the same rounded shapes and soft palette so they work together as a set.
