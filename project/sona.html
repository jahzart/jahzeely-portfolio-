Sona Strategic Advisors

Logo design for Sona Strategic Advisors, a private capital firm that provides credit and equity backed by tangible assets.

Sona means gold in Hindi. The symbol joins an S and an O in one continuous stroke that loops like an evergreen cycle, where lender and shareholder meet.

Client
Sona Strategic Advisors
Service
Logo
Year
2026

Gold, in a single continuous line

The palette pairs deep navy with a matte gold that feels like metal or an ingot rather than shine, giving the brand a calm, solid and trustworthy presence.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Photoshop

Creative process

  1. 1. Brief and concept. Research on the firm, its investment model and the meaning of the name to define the core idea.
  2. 2. Proposals. Four logo directions presented with variations, typography, palette and mockups.
  3. 3. Final identity. Refinement of the chosen symbol and delivery of the full set of versions for light, dark and gold backgrounds.
