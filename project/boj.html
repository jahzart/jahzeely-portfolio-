Beauty of Joseon Green Plum

A conceptual campaign for the Beauty of Joseon Green Plum Refreshing Cleanser, created with AI: fresh, clean photography and animated loops.

A light, low pH gel cleanser with 24% plum water and 3% mung bean seed extract that gently removes impurities while keeping the skin balanced and comfortable, without tightness.

Client
Beauty of Joseon (concept)
Service
AI Photography & Video
Year
2026

A fresh start, every day

The art direction follows the product itself: mint water, transparent gel, foam and fresh green plums, combined in five looks, from a spa bathroom and rippling water to a cobalt still life with fluted glass and orchids, and a scientific key visual with molecules.

Tools used
  • + Magnific
  • + Nano Banana Pro
  • + Kling
  • + Photoshop
Photography

Creative process

  1. 1. Moodboard. References of water, gel textures and minimal still lifes.
  2. 2. AI photography. Five photorealistic looks with the real bottle and label as reference.
  3. 3. Animation. Vertical loops for reels and stories.
