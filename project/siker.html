Siker Nutrition

Bold and energetic brand identity for a sports nutrition company targeting active, performance-driven individuals.

Siker Nutrition needed a brand that could stand out in the intensely competitive sports nutrition market. The identity had to communicate power, scientific credibility and modern lifestyle appeal simultaneously.

Client
Siker
Service
Branding
Year
2021
View on Behance

Fuel your performance

The result is a high-contrast, bold visual system with a custom wordmark, dynamic motion graphics and packaging that commands attention on shelf and screen alike.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Photoshop
  • + After Effects

Creative process

  1. 1. Market Research. Analysis of the sports nutrition category, target athlete profiles and shelf positioning strategy.
  2. 2. Identity & Motion. Wordmark design, visual system and motion graphics that bring the brand to life across digital channels.
  3. 3. Packaging Design. Product line packaging for multiple supplements with strong shelf presence and consistent brand language.
