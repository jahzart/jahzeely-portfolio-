RoverPass

New logo for RoverPass, an all-in-one campground and RV park management software.

The symbol builds the letters R and P into the silhouette of an RV, with a wheel that becomes the counter of the P. It is simple enough to work as an app icon and strong enough to lead the brand on billboards.

Client
RoverPass
Service
Logo
Year
2025

An R that is also an RV

The system includes horizontal and stacked versions, the isotype and color variations in coral, navy and white for every background.

Tools used
  • + Adobe Illustrator
  • + Photoshop

Creative process

  1. 1. Research. Review of the previous brand and the outdoor hospitality market.
  2. 2. Symbol. Exploration of letterforms and RV shapes until the R, P and wheel worked as one mark.
  3. 3. System. Lockups, color versions and applications for digital and outdoor advertising.
